Know the places first

Before anyone votes on a route or a stay — here's what's actually at each stop, and what there is to do there. Scroll through this once as a group, then go make the calls below.

Landing base

Phuket · Patong / Karon

Where the flight lands and where the party energy is. Patong is a 3km beach strip backed by Bangla Road's bars and clubs — everything is walkable, watersports operators line the sand by day, and the nightlife runs late. Karon next door is the quieter, more beach-club version of the same coastline.

2 nights on both routes
Islands + cliffs

Krabi · Ao Nang & Railay

Ao Nang is Krabi's beach town — calmer than Patong, with a beachfront strip of restaurants and bars and longtail boats leaving constantly for the islands. A 10-minute boat ride away, Railay is a cliff-locked peninsula reachable only by water, famous for rock climbing routes that rise straight out of the sand.

2 nights on both routes
Jungle reset

Khao Sok · Cheow Lan Lake

An inland detour into 160-million-year-old rainforest, ringed by dramatic limestone karsts rising straight out of the water. The overnight tour puts you in a floating bungalow on the lake itself, with a jungle trek, a cave, kayaking and boat safaris built into the two days — patchy signal, that's the point.

1 night, both routes
Day trip

Phi Phi Islands

Not a stay — a speedboat day trip from Phuket (or Krabi). Home to Maya Bay, the cove made famous by "The Beach," plus coral reef snorkel stops and a viewpoint over the twin bays. This is the one everyone's seen in photos before ever landing in Thailand.

Day trip from Phuket
